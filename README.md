Solmar Bay: Sunset Streets — an open-world crime game built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay police, and finish Rico's four jobs. All art, story, names and sounds are original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network: every file the game loads sits under game/.

## Controls

WASD move / drive · Mouse look (click to lock) · E get in / out of a car · F punch · 1 pistol, click to shoot, R reload · Shift run · P pause · M mute

## How it plays

- A 6×6-block city with a marina, docks, plaza and beach, plus traffic and pedestrians.
- Wanted level (0–5 stars): crimes bring police pursuit cars that chase along the road grid, ram you, and bust you when they corner you.
- Four story missions for Rico, with waypoint beacons and minimap markers, paying cash.
- Combat on foot (fists and pistol), car damage and wrecks, health, and a save that keeps your cash and progress in localStorage.
